Reading together in Kids Club

Kids Club offers illustrated English stories for ages 6–10 with a parent or guardian's supervision. Read along, translate unfamiliar words, save vocabulary, and practice together. Stories and illustrations are AI-generated and human-reviewed for age appropriateness before publication; choose stories that suit your child.

Stories download from our servers. There are no child accounts, profiles, or age collection. Kids Club words and progress stay on the device, separate from adult learning data. Removing a story download preserves its learning progress; clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local data. Limited pseudonymous technical analytics are explained in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a book?

Tap the add (+) button in your library and choose an EPUB or PDF file, or share a file to Coral Reader from another app. The book is imported and saved on your device.

Where are my books stored?

Imported files stay on your device; they are not uploaded as files or full chapters. Optional adult cloud AI sends selected text and limited reading context for translation or enrichment. Kids Club uses local translation. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, local books and learning data may be removed.

How do I change the theme, font, or language?

Open Settings to switch between light, dark, and system themes and to change the app language. While reading, open the reading options to adjust font size, spacing, and the page appearance.

What is included in a subscription, and is there a free trial?

An optional subscription unlocks premium features and is available as a monthly or yearly plan, sometimes with a free trial. Billing is handled by your app store (Apple App Store or Google Play).

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your app store account:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Coral Reader.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Coral Reader.

If you cancel, you keep premium access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same app store account you used to subscribe.

How do I turn off analytics or cloud AI?

Open Settings and turn off “Share anonymous usage data.” This stops Firebase and Mixpanel analytics and Crashlytics reporting; the rest of the app keeps working normally. The cloud AI opt-out in Settings is a separate control for optional adult cloud translation and enrichment. Turning off analytics does not turn off cloud AI requests.

I found a bug or my book won’t open — what should I do?

Email yanayh90@gmail.com with your device model, OS version, and a short description (and the file type, if a specific book is affected). We’ll help.
