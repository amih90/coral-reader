Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 18, 2026

No account, login, name, email, or age is required. Imported book files stay on your device; we do not upload full files or chapters. Optional adult cloud AI sends selected excerpts and limited context to our backend when used. Kids Club translation, saved words, and progress stay local, separate from adult learning data. Stories download from our servers. We use limited pseudonymous technical data for app functionality and reliability, not advertising or profiling. We do not sell your data or track you across apps or websites.

No identity information does not mean no technical data. App identifiers, network metadata, diagnostics, and purchase records can be pseudonymous personal data without a name or account. The processing below includes Firebase, Mixpanel, RevenueCat, and our Azure-backed services.

1. Who we are & how to contact us

The app “Coral Reader” is published by Yanai Hollander, an individual sole proprietor resident in Israel, who acts as the data controller for the limited data described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, email yanayh90@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Coral Reader mobile app on Android and iOS, including data processed on your device, parent-supervised Kids Club for ages 6–10, story downloads, optional adult cloud AI, and the third-party providers listed in Section 6.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No user accounts, logins, or passwords; no name, email address, username, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We do not upload imported book files or full chapters. Optional adult cloud AI processes selected excerpts and limited context as described below, not your entire library.
  • No child accounts, profiles, names, dates of birth, or ages. Kids Club saved words, answers, and learning progress are not uploaded to or stored on our backend.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for cross-context behavioral advertising or for tracking you across other apps or websites.

5. Why we process it & legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

  • To provide and operate the app: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To manage subscriptions and verify entitlements: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To deliver stories and optional cloud language results: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To understand whether app features work and improve reliability, not for advertising or child profiling: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) where required, otherwise our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can withdraw consent at any time via the Settings opt-out described in Section 10.
  • To diagnose crashes and keep the app stable and secure: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Third-party providers

We rely on a small number of established providers that process data on our behalf or as independent controllers for billing. Each maintains its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — usage analytics (firebase.google.com/support/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  • Mixpanel — app functionality analytics (mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
  • Microsoft Azure — backend infrastructure, catalog delivery, and optional cloud AI processing with the configured model provider (privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
  • The configured AI model provider processes the limited optional cloud AI payload described in Section 4. Contact us for the current provider and applicable terms.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and receipt validation (revenuecat.com/privacy).
  • Apple App Store — payment processing and subscription billing on iOS (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • Google Play — payment processing and subscription billing on Android (policies.google.com/privacy).

7. International data transfers

Some providers are based in the United States or operate globally, so your data may be transferred outside Israel, the EEA, or the UK. Where this happens, the transfer relies on the providers’ safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or applicable adequacy decisions.

8. Data retention

Data stored on your device persists until you remove it (delete a book, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app). Analytics, crash, and subscription data are retained according to the providers’ configured retention windows and then deleted or aggregated. Backend and AI model provider data are retained under the applicable service configuration and provider terms; we do not promise zero retention for these services.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, and the California CCPA/CPRA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to processing, and to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Because Coral Reader has no accounts, most data is either stored only on your device or is pseudonymous telemetry keyed to a device/instance identifier. To make a request, email yanayh90@gmail.com; we may ask for information needed to locate the relevant identifiers.

10. Analytics consent & opt-out

Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, and Crashlytics are enabled by default. You can turn them off at any time using the “Share anonymous usage data” toggle in Settings. When you turn it off, analytics and crash collection stop, and essential app functionality is unaffected.

The cloud AI opt-out in Settings is separate. Disabling analytics does not disable optional cloud AI requests or the network requests needed to download stories and manage purchases.

11. Children’s privacy & Kids Club

Kids Club is an English-reading experience designed for ages 6–10 with parent or guardian supervision. This is a recommended reading range, not age verification; we do not ask for or store children's ages. There are no child accounts or profiles.

Saved words, practice results, and reading progress stay on the device, separate from adult learning data, and are not stored on our backend. Removing a downloaded story preserves local progress; clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local learning data. Stories are downloaded from our servers, not uploaded from the device.

Kids Club translation runs locally and does not use adult cloud enrichment. Limited pseudonymous functionality events and technical download metadata described in Section 4 may still be processed. We do not use them for advertising or child profiling. Parents can manage local data and the settings in Section 10, or contact us about privacy concerns.

Stories and illustrations are AI-generated and human-reviewed for age appropriateness before being added to the app. Review is not a guarantee that every story suits every child; parents should choose stories and supervise use.

12. Do Not Sell or Share (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not process it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is therefore nothing to opt out of in that respect, but you may still use the analytics opt-out in Section 10.

13. App store privacy labels & tracking

Consistent with this policy, the data we collect is classified as Usage Data, Diagnostics, and Purchases, is not linked to your identity, and is not used to track you. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or any advertising SDKs, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is required on iOS.

14. Security

We use local-first, on-device storage for your books, encrypt data in transit to our providers using TLS, store no account credentials, and minimize and pseudonymize the telemetry we collect.

15. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where required, surface the change inside the app so you can review it.
