Need help redeeming? ↗
Who's eligible?

New subscribers to Coral Pro's monthly subscription only. Yearly and lifetime plans aren't included. This offer doesn't stack with the normal seven-day introductory trial. Store eligibility rules apply, separately for Apple and Google; the same eligible store account can't redeem it repeatedly.

What happens after the free period?

You get one calendar month free on Apple or 30 days free on Google Play, beginning when you activate the offer. Your subscription then automatically renews at the standard localized monthly price unless cancelled through your store's subscription settings before renewal. Your store shows the exact price and next billing date before confirmation.

When does the offer end?

Redeem through December 31, 2026: Google Play closes at 23:59 GMT on December 31; Apple's offer expires at 00:00 Pacific on January 1, 2027, so it remains valid through December 31 Pacific. There are 2,000 redemptions per store, subject to availability. This is the campaign limit, not a live remaining count.

My code isn't working. What should I check?

Check that you're using TELEGRAM30 with an eligible store account and the monthly plan. On Android, enter it inside the app's native Google Play monthly checkout, not the Play Store's general redeem screen. A newly created Apple offer may take time to appear. If the free period isn't shown, don't confirm a paid purchase expecting the code to apply later. Contact support if you need a hand.
