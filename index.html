Private by default

Your books stay yours.

Your library lives on your device. Translation runs with downloaded on-device language models, so the passages you select are never sent to a translation service. Read Aloud uses on-device voices.

  • No account. Import a book and start reading.
  • Manage downloaded translation models inside the app; downloads over cellular are off by default.
  • Machine translation is a comprehension aid, not a professional translation.
